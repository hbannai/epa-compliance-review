✅ EPA Compliance Checks Performed by AI

Every submitted document is analyzed by Amazon Bedrock (Mistral AI) against the following 18 compliance checkpoints based on EPA Compliance Monitoring regulations.

🌊 Clean Water Act (CWA)

1. NPDES Permit Compliance
Discharge monitoring reports (DMRs), effluent limitations, permit conditions
2. Water Quality Standards
Pollutant concentrations vs. EPA water quality criteria
3. Discharge Reporting
Completeness of discharge monitoring data & reporting frequency
4. Pretreatment Standards
Industrial pretreatment program compliance for indirect dischargers

💧 Safe Drinking Water Act (SDWA)

5. Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs)
Reported contaminant levels vs. EPA MCL standards
6. Monitoring & Reporting
Required monitoring schedules & public notification requirements
7. Treatment Techniques
Compliance with required treatment technologies

🚬 Effluent & Discharge

8. Effluent Limitation Guidelines (ELGs)
Industry-specific effluent limits
9. TMDL Compliance
Total Maximum Daily Load allocations
10. Stormwater Management
Stormwater pollution prevention plan (SWPPP) compliance

🏭 Facility & Operations

11. Record Keeping
Required records maintained and accessible per inspection
12. Self-Monitoring Data
Facility self-monitoring data completeness & accuracy
13. Spill Prevention (SPCC)
Spill Prevention, Control & Countermeasure plan compliance
14. Best Management Practices
Implementation of required BMPs

📈 Reporting & Transparency

15. Timely Reporting
Reports submitted within regulatory deadlines
16. Data Accuracy
Inconsistencies, anomalies, or suspicious data patterns
17. Public Notification
Compliance with public notification requirements
18. Corrective Actions
Corrective action plans for prior violations

How AI Flagging Works

  • Risk Score > 70 or Priority URGENT/CRITICAL → FLAGGED FOR REVIEW
  • Risk Score 41-70 or Priority ELEVATED → NEEDS REVIEW
  • Risk Score ≤ 40 and Priority ROUTINE → AI APPROVED
