CITIZEN COMPLAINT - WETLANDS DESTRUCTION
Reporter: Chesapeake Watershed Alliance
Date: March 28, 2026
Location: Lot 45-B, Riverside Commerce Park, Anne Arundel County, MD
GPS: 38.9784° N, 76.5412° W

OBSERVATIONS:
Approximately 4.5 acres of jurisdictional wetlands have been filled
with construction debris, soil, and concrete rubble WITHOUT a Clean
Water Act Section 404 permit.

Evidence collected:
- Aerial photos from March 2025 showing intact forested wetland
- Current photos showing complete fill and grading
- No Army Corps of Engineers permit on file (confirmed via USACE)
- No state wetland permit issued (confirmed via MDE)

IMPACT:
- Wetland provided flood attenuation for downstream residential area
- Habitat for state-listed endangered Bog Turtle documented in 2023
- Connected to Chesapeake Bay tributary via unnamed stream
- Downstream water quality monitoring shows increased sediment and
  nutrient loading since fill activity began

RESPONSIBLE PARTY: Riverside Development Group LLC
Previous CWA violations: 2 (2021, 2023) — unpaid penalties totaling $45,000

RECOMMENDATION: Emergency restoration order + penalties.
