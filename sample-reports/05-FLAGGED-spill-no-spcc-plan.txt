SPILL INCIDENT REPORT
Facility: Lakeshore Petroleum Storage Terminal
Date of Spill: March 5, 2026
Substance: No. 2 Fuel Oil
Volume Released: Approximately 8,500 gallons

DETAILS:
A corroded underground storage tank (UST-7, installed 1985) failed,
releasing approximately 8,500 gallons of No. 2 fuel oil into the
surrounding soil. Contamination has reached the groundwater table
at 12 feet below grade. The facility is located 300 feet from
Lake Marion, a public drinking water source.

SPCC PLAN STATUS:
- The facility does NOT have a current SPCC plan.
- Last SPCC plan was prepared in 2008 and never updated.
- No secondary containment exists for UST-7.
- Facility never conducted integrity testing on the 41-year-old tank.

RESPONSE:
- Spill was discovered by a neighboring property owner, not facility staff.
- Facility did not report the spill — it was reported by the neighbor.
- EPA notification occurred 72 hours after discovery (required: immediate).
- No cleanup contractor has been engaged as of report date.

PRIOR VIOLATIONS: 3 previous spill violations in 2020, 2022, 2024.
