CITIZEN COMPLAINT - UNAUTHORIZED DISCHARGE
Reporter: Anonymous Concerned Citizen
Date of Observation: March 22, 2026, approximately 11:45 PM
Location: Blackwater Creek, downstream of Henderson Industrial Park
GPS: 37.4085° N, 79.1425° W

OBSERVATION:
I observed a tanker truck from what appeared to be Henderson Metal Works
dumping a dark liquid directly into Blackwater Creek via an unpermitted
outfall pipe behind the facility. The discharge had a strong chemical
odor and produced a visible sheen on the water surface.

The next morning (March 23), I observed dead fish along approximately
200 yards of the creek downstream from the discharge point. The water
had a dark brown/orange discoloration.

Photos and video evidence attached (4 files).

This facility does NOT appear to have an NPDES permit for this outfall.
I checked EPA ECHO database — no active permits found.

Previous complaint filed January 2026 — no response received from EPA.
