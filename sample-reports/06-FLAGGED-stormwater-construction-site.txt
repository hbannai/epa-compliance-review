Permit: General NPDES Construction Stormwater Permit
Inspector: Regional Water Board Staff
Date: March 10, 2026

FINDINGS:
1. SWPPP STATUS: NO Stormwater Pollution Prevention Plan on site.
   Contractor unable to produce any stormwater documentation.

2. EROSION CONTROLS: NONE installed despite active grading on
   steep slopes (>15% grade). Required silt fences, fiber rolls,
   and stabilized construction entrances are absent.

3. SEDIMENT DISCHARGE: Active sediment plume observed entering

   and concrete washout observed with no secondary containment.
   Materials stored within 50 feet of the creek.

   Permit requires weekly visual monitoring and sampling during
   storm events.

6. BMPs: ZERO best management practices implemented.

RECOMMENDED ACTION: Immediate Stop Work Order.
