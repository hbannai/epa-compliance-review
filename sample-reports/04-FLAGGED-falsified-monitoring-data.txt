DATA INTEGRITY INVESTIGATION REPORT
Facility: Pacific Coast Refinery
NPDES Permit #: CA0054892
Investigation Date: February 2026

FINDINGS:
During routine record review, the following data integrity issues
were identified:

1. DUPLICATE ENTRIES: Identical BOD and TSS values reported for
   12 consecutive days (Jan 5-16, 2026) — statistically impossible.
   All values reported as exactly 18.5 mg/L BOD, 24.0 mg/L TSS.

2. BACKDATED RECORDS: Laboratory analysis dates precede sample
   collection dates for 8 samples in the January reporting period.

3. MISSING CHAIN OF CUSTODY: No chain of custody documentation
   exists for any samples collected in Q4 2025.

4. CALIBRATION RECORDS: Flow meter calibration records show the
   device was last calibrated in 2022 — 4 years overdue.

5. OPERATOR TESTIMONY: Facility operator stated "we just copy last
   month's numbers when the lab results don't come back in time."

RECOMMENDATION: Refer to EPA Criminal Investigation Division.
This constitutes potential falsification under 18 U.S.C. § 1001.
